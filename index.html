THE IDEA

Predict the next BEV latent.

01 / REPRESENTATION

One world, shared across
space, time, and action.

The world evolves in a unified BEV latent. Images are geometry-conditioned observations of that world, while the action expert learns to plan alongside its evolution.

01

Learn the spatial world

Multi-view reconstruction provides self-supervision for a common BEV space.

02

Roll the world forward

Model future BEV states with spatial and causal temporal attention, supported by a KV cache.

03

Learn to act in it

Train world dynamics and the action expert together to connect future context with driving trajectories.

02 / PHYSICAL GEOMETRY

Change the observation.
Keep the world.

Camera calibration and ego poses have physical meaning. A shared BEV representation can be decoded with different geometric parameters.

01 — ROTATE THE CAMERA

Look from a new viewpoint.

Hold the BEV latent fixed and rotate the camera extrinsics at decoding time to generate a new view.

Schematic interaction · not live model inference

02 — TRANSLATE THE EGO POSE

A different path through the same world.

Shift the historical ego poses at decoding time to synthesize observations along a translated trajectory. The demo illustrates local spatial and temporal consistency.

03 / WORLD PREDICTION + PLANNING

Imagine what comes next.
Decide where to go.

The same model predicts future BEV states and driving trajectories across datasets. Each dataset’s camera configuration decodes the shared future into its own observations.

FUTURE CONTEXT → ACTION CHOICES

Multiple plausible futures.
Multiple possible trajectories.

World dynamics provide future context to the action expert. Trajectory multimodality reflects the complexity of the driving scene.

04 / CROSS-CONFIGURATION TRANSFER

A shared world beyond
a single camera rig.

Future BEV states can be decoded using a different vehicle’s camera configuration. This separates the world representation from a particular set of camera observations.

05 / TRAINING & EVALUATION

Diverse observations.
A common representation.

Approximately 200,000 clips from multiple vehicles and camera setups contribute to one BEV space, connecting multi-view pretraining with driving adaptation.
